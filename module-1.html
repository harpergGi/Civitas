In this initial lesson, we cover the strict legal parameters surrounding 3-Day, 30-Day, and 60-Day eviction notices in the state of California. As a Legal Document Assistant, your role is to ensure these documents are filled out with absolute precision according to the client's instructions.

We will review common procedural errors that cause notices to be thrown out in court, and how to verify that the dates of service comply with state law timelines.
