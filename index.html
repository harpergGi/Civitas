Your Protections

The laws that are
already on your side.

Federal

Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA)

You have the right to dispute inaccurate, incomplete, or unverifiable information. Bureaus must investigate within 30 days. Civitas prepares your dispute to meet these statutory requirements.

Federal

Fair Debt Collection Practices Act

Debt collectors must validate debts upon written request. They cannot harass or misrepresent. Civitas prepares your validation letters within statutory timeframes to preserve your rights.

California

CA Civil Code / Tenant Rights

California tenants have strong rights regarding security deposits, habitability, and retaliation. Civitas prepares correspondence that cites the applicable code sections directly.

Federal

Credit Repair Organizations Act

Any company providing credit services must give you a written contract, a three-day right to cancel, and cannot charge upfront fees. These protections govern how we operate.
