Your Protections

The laws that are
already on your side.

Most people who contact us do not know these statutes exist. We do. And we know how to use them to prepare documentation that speaks the right language.

Federal

Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA)

You have the right to dispute inaccurate, incomplete, or unverifiable information on your credit report. Bureaus must investigate within 30 days. Furnishers must correct errors. Civitas prepares your dispute documentation to meet these statutory requirements precisely.

Federal

Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (FDCPA)

Debt collectors must validate debts upon written request. They cannot harass, threaten, or misrepresent. You have a 30-day validation window after first contact. Civitas prepares your validation letters within these statutory timeframes so your rights are fully preserved.

Federal

Credit Repair Organizations Act (CROA)

Any company providing credit services must give you a written contract, a three-day right to cancel, and cannot charge fees before services are performed. These protections apply to you as our client and govern how we operate, without exception.

California

CA Civil Code, Tenant Protections

California tenants have some of the strongest rights in the nation, including habitability standards, security deposit limits and timelines, retaliation protections, and required notice periods. Civitas prepares correspondence that cites the applicable code sections directly.
